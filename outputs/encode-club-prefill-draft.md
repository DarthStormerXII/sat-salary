**Short Description:** BTC-collateral payroll streams on Mezo — agencies keep BTC exposure while streaming MUSD salary to contractors.

**Long Description:**
Sat Salary is a BitcoinFi payroll treasury for small agencies built on Mezo. The employer retains BTC as collateral, opens a BTC-backed MUSD credit line, and streams MUSD payroll to contractors in real time. The operator can start, pause, and resume individual worker streams, repay debt to improve the risk band, and stress-test the collateral ratio. SatSalaryVault is deployed on Mezo Testnet with full Foundry contract tests covering collateral recording, credit opening, payroll funding, stream withdrawal, pause/resume, and debt repayment. The UI demo uses clearly labeled fixture data for treasury state, while the proof section shows the live deployed contract address and Mezo RPC probe.
